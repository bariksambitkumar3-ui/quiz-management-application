About

Our examination platform connects schools, teachers and students. Teachers schedule quizzes, students take them on any device, and everyone gets clear, instant performance insights.

Features

🗓️

Scheduled quizzes

Quizzes open and close at the time the teacher sets.

⏱️

Timed & auto-submit

A countdown timer, with answers saved and submitted when time ends.

✅

Instant marks

Marks, percentage and pass or fail appear right after submitting.

📊

Performance charts

Subject-wise results, averages and progress over time.

👩‍🏫

Teacher tools

MCQ, true/false and short-answer questions with class analysis.

🔒

Secure access

Separate admin, teacher and student logins.
